Fuel Tracking System

A Fuel Tracking System is a tool used to monitor and manage fuel usage in vehicles or equipment. It helps track fuel consumption, refueling activities, and mileage to reduce fuel waste, detect possible fuel theft, and improve efficiency. These systems are commonly used in fleet management and often include features like GPS tracking, real-time data reports, and alerts.
